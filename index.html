Last One Down

Race your friends to the lodge while something comes down the mountain behind you. You don't have to be fast. You have to be faster than the slowest skier.

A mountain is four bowls with a chairlift between them. Everyone starts each bowl together; points decide who wins the mountain.

What's chasing you?
Phone: hold and drag left or right of your skier to carve. Tap the round button to use an item, POLE to jab whoever is beside you. Hold CHARGE before a ramp for more air. In the air, flick up, down, left or right as many times as you can for tricks.
Keyboard: ← → steer, ↓ tuck, ↑ brake, Space uses an item, F jabs your pole, hold Shift to charge a jump, C changes the camera.
Controller: left stick or d-pad steers, A (hold) charges a jump, X jabs your pole, B uses an item, RT tuck, LT brake.
Pole jab: knock over a skier right beside or just ahead of you and get a speed boost. Miss and you wobble. 3 s to recharge.
Charge jump: hold Shift / A / CHARGE on the way to a ramp. You can't turn while charging, and any hit cancels it. A full charge (1.5 s) sends you much higher.
Tricks: in the air, mash ← → (spin), ↑ (backflip), ↓ (grab) as many times as you can. Mixing them up is worth more than repeating one. Land clean for a speed boost; still mid-move when you hit the snow? You bail.
Camera: tap the camera button (bottom left) for Chase, High, Overhead, Skier's eye or Classic 2D.
Hit the yellow crates for ⚡ Speed Wax, ❄️ Snowball Cannon, or 🎯 Bullseye Flare, which marks the racer ahead so the chaser goes after them.
